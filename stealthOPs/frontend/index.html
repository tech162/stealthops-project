STEALTHOPS

Steal the treasure. Slip past the guard. Escape.

Moves left 35
Treasure Not collected

Use W A S D or the arrow keys to move.

Mission

Collect the treasure and reach the exit within 35 moves. The exit is locked until you have the treasure.

Legend

  • 🥷 Player
  • 💂 Guard (patrols left and right)
  • 📷 Security camera
  • 💎 Treasure
  • 🚪 Exit
  • Wall
  • Camera's line of sight

Controls

  • W ↑ Up
  • S ↓ Down
  • A ← Left
  • D → Right
  • R Restart

Rules

  • The guard moves one step after every move you make.
  • If the guard ends up next to you, you are caught.
  • If the camera sees you in its row, you are caught.
  • Walls block the camera's view.
  • Bumping into a wall does not use a move.
